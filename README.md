#  Credit Card Financial Dashboard 

##  Project Overview
This project provides a Power BI Dashboard for analyzing credit card transactions, customer demographics, and revenue insights. The reports offer interactive visualizations to help financial institutions understand customer spending behavior and revenue streams.

##  Tools & Technologies Used
- Power BI (For data visualization & reporting)
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** (For calculated measures & KPIs)
- SQL & Excel (For data extraction & transformation)
- Data Modeling (Building relationships between tables)
- Interactive Dashboards (Filters, slicers, and drill-downs)

##  Key Insights & Results
### Transaction Report
1. Revenue Breakdown: Analyzed revenue by expenditure type (bills, groceries, travel, etc.).
2. Transaction Count & Volume: Evaluated total transactions and amounts processed.
3. Interest & Fees Analysis: Summarized revenue from interest and annual fees per card type.
4. Customer Job Segmentation: Analyzed revenue distribution across different professions.
5. Payment Method Insights: Tracked chip, swipe, and online transactions.

### Customer Report
6. Demographic Analysis: Revenue distribution by gender, age group, and education level.
7. State-Wise Revenue: Identified top states contributing to total revenue.
8. Income & Spending Trends: Correlated customer income levels with credit card usage.
9. Card Category Performance: Compared revenue contribution from Blue, Silver, Gold, and Platinum cards.
10. Customer Segmentation: Grouped customers based on dependent count, salary levels, and spending patterns.



##  How to Use
1. Open the Power BI dashboards or PDFs to explore financial insights.
2. Use filters & slicers for interactive data analysis.
3. Modify or extend DAX measures & visualizations as needed.
